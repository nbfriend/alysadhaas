About Alysa

Therapy rooted in warmth & evidence

I'm Alysa Dhaas, a Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist with a deep commitment to creating a safe, non-judgmental space for every person who walks through my door — or logs onto my screen.

My approach is collaborative and integrative, drawing from Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), Internal Family Systems (IFS), and mindfulness-based practices. I believe healing happens in relationship, and I am honored to walk alongside you.

I am LGBTQ+ affirming and welcoming of all relationship structures, cultural backgrounds, and identities.

  • Licensed Marriage & Family Therapist (LMFT)
  • M.S. in Marriage and Family Therapy
  • Trained in Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)
  • Internal Family Systems (IFS) Level 1 Trained
  • Member, American Association for Marriage & Family Therapy
