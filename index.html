About Alysa

Therapy that centers curiosity, compassion, and trust in your own process.

Alysa Haas is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor Associate offering virtual therapy for clients in Washington State. She holds an M.A. in Clinical Mental Health Counseling, completed Internal Family Systems Level 1 training, and brings a BFA in Modern Dance plus years of movement teaching into her work.

  • Collaborative sessions that meet you with warmth, steadiness, and respect.
  • An affirming, trauma-aware approach for people navigating overwhelm, old wounds, and change.
  • Support that makes space for both emotional insight and embodied awareness.

Approach

Healing work that follows your pace instead of forcing a script.

Alysa’s approach emphasizes safety, consent, and the belief that meaningful change often starts by listening to what your internal system is already communicating.

  • Internal Family Systems as a central framework for compassionate self-understanding.
  • Trauma-informed care that avoids judgment and supports organic healing.
  • Practical support for integrating insight into everyday relationships, work, and rest.
